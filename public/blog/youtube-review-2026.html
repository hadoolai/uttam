YouTube Review 2026: Monetization, Shorts, Earnings & Complete Guide

An original, practical guide to YouTube for beginners, creators and businesses.

Updated September 6, 2026 • By HadoolAI Editorial Team

YouTube remains one of the strongest video platforms for creators in 2026. Its combination of search, recommendations, Shorts, long-form video, live streaming and creator monetization makes it useful for both beginners and established channels.

This guide explains YouTube from a creator perspective: how YPP works, current eligibility, Shorts versus long-form revenue, AI content, reused content, copyright, analytics and a practical growth strategy.

Important: YouTube does not guarantee a fixed payment for a particular number of views. Earnings vary by audience, advertising demand, geography, content and monetization system.

1. What Is YouTube?

YouTube is a global video platform covering entertainment, education, gaming, technology, music, reviews, tutorials, travel, business and almost every other content category.

For creators, YouTube can become a searchable content library, community and business asset. A useful tutorial or review can continue attracting viewers long after publication, which gives evergreen content an advantage over purely trend-driven posts.

3. YouTube Shorts

Shorts are useful for discovery and fast experimentation. A strong Short normally communicates its idea quickly, has a clear opening, provides an actual payoff and gives viewers a reason to watch rather than simply scrolling past.

Shorts revenue is not the same as long-form Watch Page revenue. YouTube uses a separate Shorts Feed advertising model based on eligible engaged views and a Creator Pool.

Good Shorts checklist

  • Original idea or meaningful creative contribution
  • Strong first seconds
  • Clear story or useful information
  • Good pacing
  • Satisfying ending

4. Long-Form Videos

Long-form videos are particularly strong for tutorials, software reviews, product comparisons, documentaries, education and detailed storytelling. The goal should not be to make a video artificially long. Make it as long as the topic genuinely needs.

Simple structure

  1. Hook and promise
  2. Context
  3. Main explanation or demonstration
  4. Examples
  5. Conclusion
  6. Relevant next step

5. What Is the YouTube Partner Program?

The YouTube Partner Program, or YPP, gives eligible creators access to monetization features and creator resources. YouTube requires applicants to follow channel monetization policies, live in an available region, have no active Community Guidelines strikes when applying, enable 2-Step Verification, have advanced features access and have an active AdSense for YouTube account linked or ready to be set up in YouTube Studio.

Reaching a numerical threshold does not automatically mean acceptance. YouTube reviews the channel as a whole.

6. YouTube Monetization Eligibility in 2026

For the higher YPP route that includes ad-revenue sharing, YouTube currently lists:

RouteRequirement
Long-form1,000 subscribers + 4,000 qualified public watch hours in the last 12 months
Shorts1,000 subscribers + 10 million qualified public Shorts views in the last 90 days

Watch hours from Shorts Feed views do not count toward the 4,000 qualified public watch-hour threshold. Qualified Shorts views must meet YouTube's eligibility rules.

Do not confuse threshold with approval. Every eligible application is reviewed for compliance with YouTube policies.

7. How Much Does YouTube Pay?

There is no universal fixed payment for 1,000 views. Revenue depends on factors such as audience location, advertising demand, content category, monetized playbacks and the applicable agreement.

YouTube's current partner earnings documentation says the Watch Page Monetization Module pays creators 55% of net revenues from ads displayed or streamed on public videos on the Watch Page, subject to the applicable agreement.

For fan-funding features covered by the Commerce Product Module, YouTube currently states a 70% share of net revenues for channel memberships, Super Chat, Super Stickers and Super Thanks.

These are revenue-share rates, not guarantees of a particular income level.

8. YouTube Shorts Revenue Sharing

YouTube pools revenue from ads shown between Shorts. The Creator Pool is allocated based on eligible engaged views and other factors, including music licensing arrangements.

For monetizing partners who accept the Shorts Monetization Module, YouTube currently states that creators receive 45% of the revenue allocated to them from the Shorts Creator Pool. YouTube Premium revenue allocated to Shorts creators also uses a 45% share.

Key difference: Shorts and long-form videos use different revenue systems, so their revenue per view should not be compared as if they were identical.

9. Other Ways to Earn

  • Channel memberships: recurring viewer support with eligible perks.
  • Super Thanks: viewer support on eligible content.
  • Super Chat and Super Stickers: fan support during eligible live streams and premieres.
  • YouTube Premium: eligible creators can receive a share of Premium revenue.
  • Shopping: available tools can help eligible creators connect audiences with products.
  • External business: courses, services, sponsorships or products can complement platform revenue where appropriate.

10. Original and Authentic Content

YouTube's monetization policies emphasize original and authentic content. Monetized content should be the creator's own work or meaningfully transformed when using third-party material.

YouTube's current policy also says monetized content should not be mass-produced, generic, repetitive or primarily created only to generate views.

Good examples

  • Original tutorials
  • Reviews with your own analysis
  • Documentaries with original research and narration
  • Reactions with substantial commentary
  • Transformative educational or critical content
  • Original fictional stories made with creative tools

11. Reused Content

Reused content is a major monetization issue for creators who download clips from other platforms. YouTube says some transformed reused content can be monetized when it adds significant commentary, modification or educational or entertainment value.

High-risk examples

  • Re-uploading TikTok, Instagram or YouTube videos
  • Compilations with little narration
  • Movie or TV clips with minimal original context
  • Reading online articles word-for-word
  • Mass-produced videos with tiny changes
Important: Copyright permission and reused-content monetization are separate. Even permission from the original creator does not automatically make a channel eligible for YPP.

12. Can AI-Generated Videos Be Monetized?

AI can be a useful production assistant for scripts, visuals, voice, editing and brainstorming. The final channel still needs originality, authenticity and viewer value.

YouTube's current policy specifically identifies repetitive or mass-produced content as a monetization risk. A channel publishing hundreds of near-identical AI videos can therefore face problems even if the creator technically generated every file themselves.

Better AI workflow

  1. Choose a unique topic.
  2. Research and fact-check.
  3. Write an original script.
  4. Use AI tools as production assistance.
  5. Add original narration, commentary or creative direction.
  6. Edit the result instead of uploading raw generations.
  7. Make each video materially different.

Creators should also avoid AI personas that misleadingly present themselves as qualified human experts on sensitive subjects such as medical, legal, financial or political advice.

13. YouTube Copyright

Downloading a video, image or song from the internet does not automatically give you the right to use it. Use your own footage, properly licensed assets or material you have a valid right to use.

  • Keep evidence of licenses for commercial assets.
  • Use original music or appropriately licensed music where possible.
  • Do not assume adding captions makes copied footage yours.
  • Understand that fair use is fact-specific and not blanket permission.

15. How to Grow a YouTube Channel in 2026

Choose a focused niche

A clear topic makes it easier for viewers to understand why they should subscribe.

Create content clusters

Build connected videos around related questions instead of unrelated uploads.

Improve the first seconds

Deliver the promised value quickly and remove unnecessary introductions.

Build series

When a topic works, create related videos that continue the viewer's journey.

Use Shorts strategically

Shorts can test topics and introduce new viewers, while long-form videos can provide deeper value.

Review analytics weekly

Change one variable at a time so you can learn what actually improved performance.

17. YouTube for AI Creators

AI channels can grow quickly, but speed creates a quality trap. The strongest AI channels develop recognizable characters, stories, research, narration and creative direction rather than publishing raw generated clips.

A useful formula is original idea + research + human editorial judgment + AI-assisted production + meaningful editing.

18. YouTube and Your Website

YouTube and a website can support each other. A detailed website article can explain a subject, while a YouTube video can demonstrate it. For example, a HadoolAI tool review can have a written review, tutorial video, Short and comparison video.

This creates a content ecosystem instead of depending on a single platform.

21. Frequently Asked Questions

How many subscribers are needed for YouTube monetization?

For the higher YPP route that includes ad revenue, YouTube currently lists 1,000 subscribers plus either 4,000 qualified public watch hours in the last 12 months or 10 million qualified public Shorts views in the last 90 days.

Does YouTube pay for Shorts?

Yes. Eligible partners who accept the Shorts Monetization Module can earn from eligible Shorts Feed advertising and YouTube Premium through the Shorts revenue system.

How much does YouTube pay per 1,000 views?

There is no universal fixed amount. Earnings vary by audience, advertising demand, geography, content and monetized activity.

Can AI videos be monetized?

Yes, AI can be part of production, but mass-produced, repetitive or generic content can be ineligible. The final work should provide original and authentic value.

Can reused content be monetized?

Some transformed content can qualify when it adds significant original commentary or value. Minimal changes and copied compilations can fail the reused-content policy.

Can monetization be removed?

Yes. YouTube continuously checks channels in YPP. Policy violations can result in loss of monetization, and YouTube says it may turn off monetization for channels inactive for six months or more.

23. Final Verdict: Is YouTube Worth It in 2026?

Yes, YouTube is still worth it for creators who are willing to build a real content business. Its strongest advantages are searchable videos, recommendations, Shorts, long-form content, live streaming, analytics and multiple monetization options.

The biggest challenge is low-value production. YouTube's current policies clearly emphasize original, authentic content and warn against repetitive, mass-produced and reused content. This matters even more for creators using AI tools.

If you start a channel in 2026, focus on three things: make something worth watching, make it recognizably yours, and use analytics to improve.

HadoolAI takeaway: Don't ask only “How many views will I get?” Ask “Why would someone watch this, finish it, remember it and return for the next video?”
